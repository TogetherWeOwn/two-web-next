# Kit calendar control stylesheet

The Kit calendar uses the reviewed Ember segmented links. Serve their static
CSS on the Kit calendar only; keep the existing schedule stylesheet and its
ceilings unchanged. The formatted sheet measures 1251 raw and 528 default-zlib
gzip bytes. Its first ceilings are 1536 raw and 768 gzip bytes. The raw ceiling
is tighter than the rounded 1.25× default to keep the existing allocation.
