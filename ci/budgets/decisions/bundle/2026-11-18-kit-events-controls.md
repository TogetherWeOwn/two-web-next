# Kit calendar control stylesheet

The Kit calendar uses the reviewed Ember segmented links. Serve their static
CSS on the Kit calendar only once activated. Keep the existing schedule
stylesheet and its enforced ceilings unchanged. The formatted sheet measures
1251 raw and 528 default-zlib gzip bytes against advisory targets of 1536 raw
and 768 gzip bytes. Only byte overages on this asset are advisory; a missing
asset or invalid budget still fails CI.
