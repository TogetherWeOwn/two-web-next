# Weekly event retrospective template

Copy one note for each planned event, including events that are cancelled. Keep it short and focused on learning what to try next.

## Keep this qualitative

Record observations and feedback as non-identifying, group-level themes. Do not include names, handles, member IDs, raw private messages or screenshots, sensitive details, or identifying personal circumstances. If a detail cannot be safely generalized, leave it out.

Keep attendance, RSVPs, joins, human-message counts, voice minutes, and other human-activity counts in the separate quantitative scorecard or attendance evidence record. Do not copy, estimate, or infer those counts here. This note does not replace either quantitative record.

## Copyable template

- **Event and planned date:**
- **Outcome:** Held / Cancelled
- **Purpose:** What was this event meant to offer?
- **Feedback basis:** Non-identifying organizer observation, summarized feedback theme, or none

### Observed facts

- **What worked:** What was directly observed to help?
- **What did not / friction:** What directly observed difficulty or gap occurred?
- **Other relevant observation:** (optional)

### Interpretation — hypothesis, not fact

- What might explain those observations? What remains uncertain?

### One next experiment

- **Change to try:** Make one specific change at the next event.
- **Qualitative signal:** What would you observe or hear that helps assess the change? Do not use a count.
- **Review date for the next event:** `YYYY-MM-DD`
- **Close condition:** At the review, record whether the experiment was tried and what was observed, or why it was not run. For a cancelled event, explicitly note that no activity feedback was observed.

## Synthetic examples

These examples are fictional demonstrations of the template, not event records or quantitative evidence. They contain no attendance or human-activity metrics.

### Successful event

- **Event and planned date:** Co-op puzzle night — 2026-10-10 (synthetic)
- **Outcome:** Held
- **Purpose:** Offer a shared activity with a clear way for newcomers to join.
- **Feedback basis:** Organizer observation, summarized without attribution

#### Observed facts

- **What worked:** The activity got underway after the host clarified the join step.
- **What did not / friction:** A live clarification was needed before the activity could begin.
- **Other relevant observation:** The next-event date was mentioned at the close.

#### Interpretation — hypothesis, not fact

- Showing the join step earlier may make it easier to start. This single example does not establish a broader pattern.

#### One next experiment

- **Change to try:** Post the join step before the next session.
- **Qualitative signal:** Note whether the activity can begin without a live clarification.
- **Review date for the next event:** `2026-10-17` (synthetic)
- **Close condition:** At that review, record whether the change was tried and what was observed, or why it was not run.

### Cancelled event

- **Event and planned date:** Co-op puzzle night — 2026-10-17 (synthetic)
- **Outcome:** Cancelled
- **Purpose:** Offer a short shared puzzle activity.
- **Feedback basis:** Organizer observation; no activity feedback

#### Observed facts

- **What worked:** A cancellation notice was posted before the planned start.
- **What did not / friction:** The planned activity did not take place, so there are no observations about the activity itself.
- **Other relevant observation:** No activity feedback was collected because the event did not run.

#### Interpretation — hypothesis, not fact

- The cancellation is not evidence about whether the activity would have been valuable. Its value remains unknown.

#### One next experiment

- **Change to try:** Before announcing the next date, confirm that the host plan and activity materials are ready.
- **Qualitative signal:** Note whether the session is ready to begin as planned; do not count participants or messages here.
- **Review date for the next event:** `2026-10-24` (synthetic)
- **Close condition:** At that review, record whether the check was tried and what was observed, or why it was not run.
