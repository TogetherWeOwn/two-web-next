# Historical decision 0003 · Routine merges and advisory review

On 2026-10-31 the project owner allowed routine merges once required checks
passed and review findings were addressed. This kept ordinary changes moving
after verification. CodeRabbit findings still needed a response, while its
rate-limited check stayed advisory. The 2026-11-02
[production confirmation decision](0002-production-merge-confirmation.md)
superseded the production approval rule at the time.

See the [current protection and review rules](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#branch-protection-and-required-checks)
