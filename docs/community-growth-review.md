# TWO Community growth-spend review

## Rule

Review growth spend after 8 weeks of events. Kill rule (goal verbatim): `<20 weekly-active humans after 8 weeks of events` ends growth spend. A kill ends growth spend, never the community.

The event-week boundary follows the [weekly reporting convention](community-participation-metrics.md#weekly-reporting-convention) adopted 2026-10-20: ISO weeks, Monday 00:00 UTC inclusive to next Monday 00:00 UTC exclusive, labelled `YYYY-Www` with exact UTC timestamps. This is a reporting convention, not a change to goal targets or policy. The goal still does not specify which weekly-active-human count is compared with 20 (eighth event week, eight-week mean, or another aggregation). The review must record which count was compared — the week or weeks, exact date ranges, and source — so the decision is reproducible.

A weekly-active human is a human with at least one message or at least 10 voice-minutes during the week. Bot traffic does not count.

## Evidence for the review

Use the [weekly community scorecard](community-weekly-scorecard.md#reporting-period) for the eight event weeks, with ISO week labels, exact UTC intervals, and source references for the weekly-active-human count. Assign events by UTC start instant and retain host-zone display; record any missing/no-event calendar week explicitly rather than compressing the timeline. Consecutive weeks have adjoining boundaries, including W53 → W01 (or W52 → W01 in a 52-week year). This fixes week identity only: it does not impose the four-week monetization pass/reset rule on the eight-week review or choose the count to compare with 20. Retain the scorecard's human-message, join, and event-attendance measures as context; the growth-spend threshold is weekly-active humans. Preserve missing or unavailable evidence as missing—do not treat it as zero or infer attendance from an RSVP. Do not count bot activity as human participation.

If the scorecard or its source evidence is incomplete, record that limitation in the review rather than manufacturing a threshold result. The governing goal supplies the threshold and kill scope; the scorecard supplies the measured participation evidence.

## Decision owner

The Community Manager, recorded as owner of the TWO Community goal, owns the eight-week review and records whether the existing rule applies. This assignment is limited to applying the goal's growth-spend rule; it does not authorize new spend, monetization activation, or ending the community.

## Source and scope

Sources: the active Paperclip goal “The TWO Community” and the “TWO Web Next roadmap refresh” dated 2026-10-17. The goal defines the human-participation metric, the target, the eight-week review threshold, and the kill scope. The roadmap calls for attributable evidence and a goal-review runbook.
